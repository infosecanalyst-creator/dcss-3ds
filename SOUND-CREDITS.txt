Sound credits -- dcss-3ds
=========================

Every file in source/3ds/sounds/ is listed below with its source and
licence. All clips are raw PCM (signed 16-bit little-endian, mono, 22050 Hz).
None of them is under a licence that restricts redistribution.

1. crawl-sounds pack (CC0 1.0)
------------------------------
Source:  https://github.com/nerdcorenet/crawl-sounds (by nerdcorenet)
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal -- full text in source/3ds/sounds/LICENCE-crawl-sounds.txt
Changes: converted from OGG to raw PCM and loudness-lifted by tools/gen_sounds.py.

filename is the Freesound sound ID of the original
(https://freesound.org/s/<ID>/); jinglestone mixes two Freesound clips.

2. Generated for dcss-3ds (CC0 1.0)
-----------------------------------
These replace the pack's clips of the same names, whose original source is
not recorded. They are synthesized from scratch (numpy, no samples) by
tools/synth_sounds.py. Generated for dcss-3ds, CC0 -- released into the
public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

  falldie.pcm                          long falling whoosh, heavy thud and bounce
